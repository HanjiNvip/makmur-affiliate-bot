1. What this app does

Makmur Affiliate Bot is an automation tool that helps a TikTok creator publish their own original product-review videos to their own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The app researches TikTok Shop affiliate products, generates original video concepts, runs them through quality checks, and — only with the creator's explicit authorization — uploads the finished videos.

2. Data we collect

  • TikTok open ID and user access token (scope: video.publish), granted by you through TikTok's own OAuth authorization page.
  • Creator profile information returned by TikTok's API (e.g. display name), used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video upload metadata — post IDs and upload/posting statuses returned by the Content Posting API, used to report whether an upload succeeded.

We never ask for, collect, or store your TikTok password. Authorization happens entirely on TikTok's website.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate with TikTok on your behalf and upload videos you produced to your own account;
  • Run automated quality checks before upload;
  • Show you the status and basic analytics of your own posts.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and open IDs are stored only on the creator's own server (as environment variables / encrypted configuration). They are never uploaded to third-party servers. Data is transmitted only to TikTok's official APIs over HTTPS.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third party.

6. Data retention

Access tokens are kept until they expire or until you revoke the app's access. Revoked or expired tokens are deleted.

7. Your rights

You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → authorized apps. To request deletion of any data we hold, contact us at nhanji71@gmail.com.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
