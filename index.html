Automation for TikTok Shop affiliate creators

Makmur Affiliate Bot helps creators research TikTok Shop products, plan original review-video concepts, and publish those videos to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API — only after the creator authorizes the app themselves.

🔍 Product research

The bot scans TikTok Shop affiliate offers and scores them by commission rate, GMV trend, and content fit — so creators focus on products worth reviewing.

🎬 Concept generation

For each chosen product it drafts multiple original video concepts (hook, shot list, caption) that go through automated quality checks before anything is produced.

📤 Direct posting

Finished videos are uploaded via TikTok's official Content Posting API (Direct Post). Posting never happens without the creator's own OAuth authorization.

📈 Analytics

Post performance is tracked to detect winners and losers, so the next round of concepts learns from what actually worked.

How it works

  1. Research. The bot researches TikTok Shop affiliate products and scores them by commission, demand, and review-worthiness.
  2. Create. It generates original video concepts per product and runs them through quality checks.
  3. Authorize. You connect the app to your own TikTok account on TikTok's official authorization page (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  4. Publish. With your authorization in place, finished videos are uploaded to your own account via the official Content Posting API.

What the app asks for — and what it never does

  • Authorization happens entirely on TikTok's website. The app never asks for, collects, or stores your TikTok password.
  • The app uses only the scopes it needs: user.info.basic (to confirm which account is connected), video.upload and video.publish (to upload videos you produced).
  • Videos are labeled as AI-generated (is_aigc) where applicable, in line with TikTok's disclosure rules.
  • You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → authorized apps.

No reposting, no stolen footage, no automated login — ever.

FAQ

Can this app post to someone else's account?
No. You can only connect an account you own or manage, and you authorize it yourself on TikTok's website.
Does it repost other people's videos?
No. The whole pipeline is built around original concepts; reposting and stolen footage are prohibited by our Terms.
What data does the app keep?
Your TikTok open ID and access token (granted by you via OAuth), creator profile info, and video upload statuses. Tokens live only on your own server. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I disconnect?
Revoke access anytime in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → authorized apps). Revoked or expired tokens are deleted.
